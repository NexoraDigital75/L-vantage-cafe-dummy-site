About the concept

European mood. Hyderabad energy.

This sample homepage is designed around the qualities highlighted by current public listings: European-vintage ambience, Mediterranean touches, outdoor seating, soothing music and a broad international menu.

The visual direction is intentionally editorial rather than overly busy: large typography, warm neutrals, dark green accents and generous spacing.
